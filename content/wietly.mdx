Wietly is a social map for cannabis lovers. Find good places to light up, discover coffeeshops near you and see what your friends are smoking. Wietly is for adults (18+).

## What it does

- **A community map** of parks, benches and viewpoints, added and rated by users, with real photos
- **Coffeeshops** nearby, with opening hours and photos
- **Restricted zones** on the map, so you know where smoking isn't allowed
- **Check-ins with friends**: tag friends, log your strain and follow a feed of what your friends are doing
- **Buds, XP and ranks**: earn rewards for check-ins and new spots, climb the leaderboard and unlock profile items in the store

## How it's built

- **Expo and React Native** (New Architecture) for iOS, Android and the web
- **Mapbox** for the map, spots and restricted zones
- **Supabase**: Postgres with row-level security, auth, storage and Edge Functions for push notifications, translations and scheduled jobs
- **TanStack Query** for data fetching and caching, **PostHog** for analytics
- A **Next.js** website with shareable spot, check-in and invite pages

More on the [Wietly website](https://wietly.com).
