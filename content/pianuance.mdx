Pianuance turns a digital piano into a practice partner. Connect the piano over USB or Bluetooth MIDI, open a piece, and the score follows you as you play. Every note is marked right, wrong, early or late, and the bars that need work are picked out for you.

## What it does

- **Practice, play-along and sight-reading** modes, with loops, slow practice and a tempo ladder
- **Your own music**: import MusicXML, or turn PDFs and recordings into scores (Pro)
- **Courses, exam-style tests and ear training**, plus a teacher mode for lessons
- Runs on iPhone, iPad, Android, the web, Windows, macOS and Linux

## How it's built

- **Expo and React Native** (New Architecture) for one codebase across phones, tablets and the web
- **Verovio**, the C++ music engraving library, compiled for iOS and Android and called through JSI
- **react-native-skia** and **Reanimated** to draw the score and the moving cursor smoothly on the UI thread
- **Native MIDI modules** in Swift and Kotlin, so notes from the piano arrive with low latency
- A practice engine in plain TypeScript, separate from the UI, that matches what you play against the score
