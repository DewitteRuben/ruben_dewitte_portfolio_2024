PSSD (post-SSRI sexual dysfunction) can last long after someone stops taking an antidepressant. Besides sexual function, it can dull emotions and affect thinking and memory. That makes regular dating apps a hard place to be. PSSD Connect is a calm place where people living with PSSD can find friendship, company or a relationship, and nobody has to explain it.

## What it does

- **Discover** a few people a day, at your own pace
- **Profiles** that let people share as much or as little about their PSSD as they like
- **Chat, voice notes and calls**
- **Built-in safety tools** and human moderators
- Free to use, with an optional Plus membership
- Available in seven languages on iPhone, Android and the web

## How it's built

This is my second take on PSSD Connect. The first version (2024) was a web app on Node.js, MongoDB and Firebase. In 2026 I rebuilt it from scratch as a native app.

- **Expo and React Native** for iPhone, Android and the web from one codebase
- **Supabase**: Postgres with row-level security, auth, realtime chat and edge functions
- **LiveKit** for voice and video calls
- **RevenueCat** for subscriptions through the App Store, Google Play and Stripe on the web
- Browser walkthroughs that test the main flows end to end before each release

More at [pssdconnect.org](https://pssdconnect.org).
